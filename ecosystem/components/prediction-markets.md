# Prediction Markets

Timełess's **decentralized prediction markets** allow users to **trade on real-world event outcomes** with **blockchain-verified transparency and automated settlements**.

#### **How It Works**

- Users can **speculate on events** in **finance, politics, sports, and global affairs** by placing **wagers on possible outcomes**.
- Smart contracts **securely manage bets and payouts**, ensuring **tamper-proof resolution** without intermediaries.
- Market outcomes are determined using **trusted on-chain oracles**, preventing **manipulation or unfair settlements**.

#### **User Participation**

- **Trading Positions** – Participants can **buy and sell outcome shares**, adjusting their positions based on **real-time market shifts**.
- **Event Resolution** – Once the outcome is confirmed, the smart contract **automatically distributes winnings** to users who predicted correctly.

By eliminating **centralized control**, Timełess offers **fair, secure, and efficient prediction markets**, allowing users to **trade confidently on real-world events**.
