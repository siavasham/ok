# Immersive Gaming

**Timełess** delivers a competitive, play-to-earn Web3 gaming experience where players can test their skills or try their luck—while earning real rewards in TRDX tokens.

**Poker: Multi-Stakes Strategy**

The Timełess Poker Arena offers a fast-paced, skill-based environment with **nine stake-based tables** designed to accommodate all player levels—from casual users to high rollers.

- **Table Levels:** Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Master, Elite, Legendary, Mythic
- **Buy-ins:** Ranging from **$2 to $5,000**
- **Daily Tournaments:** Up to 4 events every day with prize pools and TRDX rewards

Players can join tournaments, compete across tiers, and earn based on performance and ranking.

**Boxes: Luck-Based Winnings**

The **Boxes** game is a high-stakes game of chance where users open mystery boxes to uncover digital rewards—or lose it all.

- **Box Outcomes:** Diamonds (win), Bombs (lose)
- **Gameplay Mechanics:** Users spend USD to open boxes with varying risk and reward probabilities
- **Earning Potential:** Lucky pulls result in instant USD and TRDX gains

By blending strategy and randomness, Timełess creates a dual-mode gaming experience that appeals to both skillful competitors and thrill-seeking players.
