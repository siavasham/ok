# Pricing Strategy

#### **Initial Liquidity Provision**

To establish a stable trading environment, Trendix will launch with an initial liquidity provision of **$10,000**, pairing **TRDX with SOL tokens**. This ensures **sufficient liquidity from the start**, allowing users to **trade TRDX seamlessly** without excessive slippage or price volatility.

#### **Initial Token Pricing**

At launch, the **initial price of TRDX** will be set at **$0.001 per token**, reflecting the liquidity provision and market entry strategy. This pricing approach ensures that **early adopters have access to the token at a fair and transparent rate**, allowing for **organic price discovery** as demand increases.

#### **Future Liquidity Plans**

To maintain **long-term price stability and market depth**, Trendix will implement:

* **Progressive Liquidity Additions** – Additional liquidity injections will be made at key milestones to **support growing trading volume and demand**.
* **Market-Making Strategies** – Automated market-making mechanisms will be employed to **reduce volatility and ensure smooth order execution**.
* **Staking & Reward-Based Liquidity Growth** – Encouraging users to **stake TRDX in liquidity pools**, further enhancing trading stability and user incentives.
* **Exchange Listings** – Expansion into additional trading platforms to **increase accessibility and token liquidity over time**.

By **strategically managing liquidity and pricing**, Trendix ensures a **sustainable token economy**, promoting **healthy market conditions and long-term adoption**.
