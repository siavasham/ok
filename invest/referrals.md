# Referral Bonus System

Invite new users to Timeless and increase your earnings through our referral tiers. Only referrals from different plans count toward your bonus level.

| Unique Referral Tiers | Bonus Multiplier |
| --------------------- | ---------------- |
| 1                     | +5%              |
| 2                     | +12%             |
| 3                     | +20%             |
| 4                     | +30%             |
| 5                     | +50%             |

> Referrals must be on **different plans** to count.
