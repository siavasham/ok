# Example Calculation

## 💼 Scenario

- **Plan**: SYNAPSE-X ($100)
- **Referrals**: INIT.AI + NEURA.ONE (2 unique plans → +12%)
- **Holding**: 10,000 $TRDX → +5%
- **Swap History**: 40% of total earnings swapped

## 🧮 Step-by-step

### Raw Earning

```text
$1.20 × (1 + 0.12 + 0.05) = $1.404
```

### Swap Damage

```text
Damage = 0.4 × 0.5 = 20%
Claimed = $1.404 × 0.80 = $1.1232/day
```

> This user earns approximately $1.12 daily after swap penalties.
