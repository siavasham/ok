# Liquidity Pool

The Timeless AI ecosystem operates on a robust and fair **$50,000 monthly liquidity pool**, designed to ensure consistent and sustainable rewards for all participants across our AI-powered economy.

![Liquidity Pool Overview](../assets/liquidity-pool-overview-placeholder.png)

---

## What is the Liquidity Pool?

The liquidity pool is a shared prize pool that funds the daily rewards paid out to users who rent GPU server instances, participate in AI training, refer others, and hold $TRDX tokens.

- **Monthly Prize Pool:** $50,000
- **Daily Distribution:** The monthly pool is divided into daily allocations (approx. $1,667 per day).
- **Fair Scaling:** If total user rewards exceed the daily allocation, rewards are scaled proportionally to maintain pool limits.
