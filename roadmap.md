# Roadmap

The Timełess roadmap outlines our phased development strategy, designed to grow the platform sustainably while delivering value to users at every step. Each phase aligns with core ecosystem milestones in AI, gaming, predictions, and token utility.

***

## ✅ Phase 1: Foundation (Q1 2024)

* Conceptualization and whitepaper release
* Formation of core team
* Smart contract architecture
* Tokenomics finalized (TRDX + Timełess token)
* Website and brand launch

***

## 🚀 Phase 2: Early Adoption (Q2 2024)

* Launch of TRDX token and initial liquidity pools
* AI tools (text-to-text, image generation) v1
* MVP of Web3 poker game
* First prediction market templates
* Community incentives and airdrops
* Strategic partnerships and advisors onboarded

***

## 🔮 Phase 3: Ecosystem Expansion (Q3 2024)

* Timełess token integration for staking and AI usage
* Full release of prediction market engine
* Launch of box-based lucky game
* Governance voting system
* Mobile-friendly web version
* AI style-transfer + avatar builder

***

## 🌍 Phase 4: Platform Maturity (Q4 2024)

* DAO infrastructure and decentralized governance
* Creator tools for AI apps and games
* TRDX cross-chain availability
* SDK/API for third-party devs
* Marketplace for AI + game assets
* First major esports poker tournament

***

## 🔧 Continuous Development

* Quarterly feature upgrades based on governance proposals
* Community-led roadmap contributions
* Ongoing scaling, optimization, and security audits
