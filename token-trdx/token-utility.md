# Token Utility

The **TRDX token** is the core utility asset of the Trendix ecosystem, enabling transactions, rewarding user actions, and driving governance across various platform features. It acts as both an incentive mechanism and a medium of exchange, facilitating engagement, participation, and value transfer within the ecosystem.

**Use Cases of TRDX**

1. **Play & Earn in Games (Web3 Games)**\
   Players earn TRDX tokens by participating in games like poker and the boxers game. In these games, users can earn rewards based on gameplay outcomes—whether it's through skillful poker strategies or luck-based box opening mechanics. Every in-game action has the potential to reward players with TRDX, promoting a true play-to-earn experience.
2. **AI-Powered Rewards**\
   Users who engage with the AI features (such as the text-to-text AI or image-to-image generator) will earn TRDX tokens. Whether interacting with the AI for daily prompts or generating unique styles for images, each action within the AI system is rewarded with TRDX tokens. This creates an additional incentive for users to engage with the platform's AI tools, further enriching the Trendix ecosystem.
3. **Governance & Platform Decisions**\
   Holders of TRDX tokens can participate in governance, proposing and voting on changes to platform features, tokenomics, and future updates. This decentralized decision-making process ensures that the platform’s evolution reflects the community’s interests and priorities.

**Why TRDX Matters**

* **Universal Rewards System:** TRDX integrates into all Trendix products, ensuring consistent earning opportunities across games, AI features, and trading activities.
* **Multi-Purpose Functionality:** It powers a range of experiences—from gaming incentives to AI-driven rewards and decentralized finance activities.
* **Sustainable Token Economy:** The mechanisms for earning and spending TRDX are designed to create a self-sustaining economy, driving both user engagement and liquidity growth.

As the primary token in the Trendix ecosystem, TRDX plays a crucial role in incentivizing users, promoting platform growth, and encouraging decentralized participation, positioning it as a valuable asset within the Web3 landscape.
