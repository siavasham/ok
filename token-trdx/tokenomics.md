# Tokenomics

#### **Total Supply**

The total supply of TRDX tokens is fixed at **100 million**, ensuring scarcity, long-term value, and controlled inflation within the Timełess ecosystem.

#### **Distribution Model**

TRDX is strategically allocated across different areas to support liquidity, ecosystem development, team incentives, marketing, staking rewards, community engagement, and airdrop campaigns.

- **Liquidity & Exchanges**\
  **10% (10 million TRDX)** – Reserved to ensure smooth trading, market stability, and efficient token circulation within decentralized and centralized exchanges.
- **Team & Development**\
  **15% (15 million TRDX)** – Allocated to fund project growth, continuous development, and long-term sustainability, ensuring that the core team remains aligned with the success of Timełess.
- **Marketing & Partnerships**\
  **10% (10 million TRDX)** – Dedicated to expanding the user base, increasing adoption, and securing strategic partnerships that contribute to the platform’s visibility and growth.
- **Staking & Rewards**\
  **24% (24 million TRDX)** – Used to incentivize platform participation, rewarding users for staking, engaging in prediction markets, playing Web3 games, and contributing to the ecosystem.
- **Community & Ecosystem Growth**\
  **20% (20 million TRDX)** – Allocated for community initiatives, governance participation, and ecosystem development, ensuring active engagement from users.
- **Reserves**\
  **20% (20 million TRDX)** – Maintained for future platform expansions, unforeseen developments, and long-term ecosystem sustainability.
- **Airdrop Campaigns**\
  **1% (1 million TRDX)** – Designated for targeted airdrop events to increase awareness, attract new users, and reward early supporters.

To promote long-term stability and prevent early token dumping, various allocations follow structured vesting schedules:

- **Team & Development** tokens are released gradually over time, ensuring alignment with key milestones and long-term project sustainability.
- **Marketing & Community Incentives** are distributed progressively to maintain steady user growth and platform adoption.
- **Staking & Rewards** are allocated based on platform activity, staking participation, and engagement levels, ensuring an ongoing incentive for users.
- **Airdrop Campaigns** are released in controlled phases, targeting specific growth milestones and community engagement goals.

By implementing **a well-balanced distribution strategy** and **structured vesting schedules**, Timełess ensures **sustainable token circulation**, fostering **platform stability and long-term ecosystem growth**.
