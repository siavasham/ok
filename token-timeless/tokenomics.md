# Timełess Tokenomics

#### **Total Supply**

The total supply of the Timełess token is fixed and carefully controlled to ensure long-term sustainability and ecosystem growth. Exact supply numbers and emission schedules are designed to balance scarcity with sufficient liquidity for ecosystem operations.

---

#### **Distribution Model**

The Timełess token distribution supports ecosystem incentives, staking rewards, development, reserves, and strategic partnerships:

| Category               | Allocation | Description                                                                            |
| ---------------------- | ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ecosystem Incentives   | 35%        | Rewards for participation in prediction markets, gaming, and AI engagement.            |
| Staking Rewards        | 25%        | Used to incentivize token staking, liquidity provisioning, and yield farming programs. |
| Development & Team     | 15%        | Supports ongoing platform development and team incentives aligned with growth.         |
| Strategic Partnerships | 10%        | Allocated to build alliances and expand platform reach.                                |
| Reserves               | 10%        | Held for future platform needs and unexpected contingencies.                           |
| Community Airdrops     | 5%         | Distributed to onboard users and reward early supporters.                              |

---

#### **Vesting & Release Schedule**

To promote ecosystem stability and user confidence, token allocations follow structured vesting and lockup periods:

- **Development & Team** tokens are vested over multiple years to ensure long-term commitment.
- **Ecosystem Incentives** and **Staking Rewards** are distributed progressively, tied to participation metrics and network growth.
- **Partnership** allocations are subject to milestone-based release schedules.
- **Community Airdrops** are phased to maximize adoption and engagement.

---

#### **Economic Role**

The Timełess token drives economic activity across the platform by:

- Facilitating value exchange in the decentralized prediction markets and gaming ecosystems.
- Supporting staking mechanisms that enhance network security and liquidity.
- Enabling governance participation on key protocol decisions.
- Promoting cross-chain interoperability to maximize ecosystem integration.
