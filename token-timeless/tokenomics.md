# Tokenomics

#### **Total Supply**

The total supply of the **Timeless Token** is fixed at **100 million**, ensuring scarcity, long-term value, and controlled inflation within the Timełess ecosystem.

#### **Distribution Model**

The Timeless Token is strategically allocated to support platform liquidity, ecosystem expansion, core team incentives, user rewards, marketing efforts, and community growth.

* **Liquidity & Exchanges**\
  **10% (10 million tokens)** – Reserved to ensure smooth trading, price stability, and efficient circulation across decentralized and centralized exchanges.
* **Team & Development**\
  **15% (15 million tokens)** – Allocated to support ongoing development, innovation, and sustainability of the platform, aligning the team with the long-term success of Timełess.
* **Marketing & Partnerships**\
  **10% (10 million tokens)** – Dedicated to user acquisition, ecosystem visibility, and forging strategic partnerships that amplify growth.
* **Staking & Rewards**\
