# Target Audience

Timełess is designed to attract a wide array of Web3 users—ranging from advanced traders to curious newcomers—by combining accessibility with deep earning potential.

## User Segments

- **Speculators & Traders**  
  Forecast outcomes on global events with provable fairness and earn TRDX for accurate predictions.

- **Web3 Gamers**  
  Play poker or chance-based box games to earn crypto through skill or luck—directly tied to the TRDX economy.

- **AI Enthusiasts & Creators**  
  Use and train AI tools (text, image, voice) while earning TRDX tokens for engagement and creativity.

- **Crypto-Native Users**  
  Join a decentralized platform with transparent earning, real governance, and versatile token use.

- **Tech-Curious Newcomers**  
  Onboard through free AI interactions and simple games, gradually unlocking the broader Web3 experience.

By blending utility, gaming, and AI into one platform, Timełess attracts a more diverse, engaged, and sticky user base than niche competitors.
